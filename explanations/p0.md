# Phase 0 — Channel characterisation
 
## Goal
 
The goal of this phase is to characterise our channel: in this case, the laptop's built-in speaker and microphone. We look at their frequency response, how they handle a pure tone, their spectrogram, etc.
 
With this, we can decide which frequencies to use (based on the SNR) and check whether the channel is reliable.
