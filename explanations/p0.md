# Phase 0 — Channel characterisation
 
## Goal
 
The goal of this phase is to characterise our channel: in this case, the laptop's built-in speaker and microphone. We look at their frequency response, how they handle a pure tone, their spectrogram, etc.
 
With this, we can decide which frequencies to use (based on the SNR) and check whether the channel is reliable.

## Results

From the measurements, I determined that the best frequency band to work with is approximately 6000 Hz to 18000 Hz. In this band the response is fairly flat (about 30–40 dB in the unnormalised FFT), and the noise floor is low and stable (about −110 dB/Hz), which gives an estimated SNR of 45–50 dB at short range.

We can also see that the mic and speakers work as expected. The spectrogram of the chirp is a single clean diagonal line.
The pure tone test confirms it: the amplitude of the recorded 10 kHz tone stays constant for the whole 5 s, so the system applies no AGC or noise suppression.
